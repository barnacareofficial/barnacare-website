About Barnacare

Where biomaterials meet biomedical research

A student-led project exploring a new concept at the intersection of skin care, biomaterials, and future wound-care technologies.

Our project

Barnacare is developing a concept for a dermal microneedle patch loaded with marine collagen. The idea brings together a microneedle-based delivery approach and a biomaterial of interest in skin and tissue research.

The project is being developed by students from the Faculty of Health Sciences at Borg El Arab Technological University. It reflects our interest in practical, research-informed biomedical innovation.

Our vision

To explore innovative biomaterial-based approaches that may contribute to future skin repair and wound-care research.


Our mission

To learn, investigate, and develop our concept through scientific thinking, collaboration, and responsible research.

Research & Innovation

The ideas behind the patch

Three connected areas guide our concept and its future research.

🌊

Marine Collagen

Investigating marine-derived collagen as a biomaterial of interest for skin-related and tissue-engineering research.

✳

Microneedle Technology

Exploring a patch-based concept that uses tiny projections designed to interact with the outer layers of the skin.

🔬

Future Wound-Care Research

Studying the concept's potential relevance to wound healing and skin regeneration through appropriate future evaluation.

Our Approach

From concept to investigation

A simple overview of the ideas we are exploring as a student team.

01

Explore the Biomaterial

Review scientific literature about marine collagen and its possible applications in biomaterials research.

02

Develop the Patch Concept

Explore how a dermal microneedle patch might be designed and what material and design questions need investigation.

03

Evaluate Responsibly

Identify the testing, safety assessment, and evidence needed before any therapeutic claims or clinical use could be considered.
